In December 2025 and January 2026, a massive collection of records known as the Epstein files began a staggered public release following the passage of the Epstein Files Transparency Act. These documents originate from federal investigations into the sex-trafficking network operated by Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell. Current Release Status (January 2026) Massive Volume: The Department of Justice (DOJ) is currently reviewing over 5.2 million pages of records, a significant increase from initial estimates. Release Timeline: Large tranches were released starting December 19, 2025. Officials have stated that because of the volume, additional documents will be released on a rolling basis, with the next major batch expected around January 20–21, 2026. Criticism of Redactions: Lawmakers and survivors have criticized the DOJ for heavy redactions, including hundreds of entirely blacked-out pages. Officials maintain these are necessary to protect the identities of over 1,200 victims.
